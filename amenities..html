About the room

The rooms are traditional Japanese-style rooms with tatami mats covering the floor. The scent of the grass used in the tatami creates a relaxing effect.

The hot springs are a natural feature of the ryokan, offering a relaxing experience in the traditional Japanese onsen style.

The ryokan offers a traditional Japanese meal experience, featuring seasonal ingredients and carefully prepared dishes.
