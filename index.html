Welcome to MOMOTAROU Hotel!

MOMOTAROU Hotel has been welcoming guests since 1987, and thanks to our wonderful guests, we have been in business for almost 40 years. Our hotel offers a beautiful open-air hot spring bath, fresh sushi prepared by our skilled sushi chef, and carefully selected Japanese sake. We will do our best to provide you with warm Japanese hospitality and help you relax and enjoy your stay. Please take your time and make yourself comfortable.
